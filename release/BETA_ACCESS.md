# Early access

Public releases are free and need no unlock code. The initial release and future
prereleases offer paid early access through Patreon. Anyone can install and
browse; video playback needs the six-digit code from that version's Patreon post.

Enter it when you press Play, or under **Patreon > Beta access** in the menu. This is separate
from your Plex sign-in code. Access is saved; a later prerelease may need a new
code. Previously unlocked releases keep working, including after cancellation.
